Cookies Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Magic Games (“we”, “us”) uses cookies on magicgamesstudio.com and similar technologies in our mobile games. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Mobile apps do not use browser cookies, but they can use similar technologies, such as software development kits (SDKs), local storage on your device, and device identifiers like the advertising ID. In this policy we call all of these “cookies” for simplicity.

2. Cookies on our website

Our website is a simple information site. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies on it, and we do not show a cookie banner because no optional cookies are set.

To display our pages we load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as part of any normal web request. Google Fonts does not set cookies. You can read more in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, pages requested and time of the request) for a short period to keep the website secure and running.

4. How to control them

  • In the game: open Settings and tap Privacy Settings, where available, to change your consent choices at any time.
  • On your device: reset or delete your advertising ID (Android: Settings > Google > Ads), or turn off app tracking (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • In your browser: you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Because our website does not set optional cookies, this will not affect how it works.
  • Industry tools: you can learn more about interest-based advertising at youronlinechoices.eu (EU) and aboutads.info (US).

5. Changes to this policy

If we start using new cookies or technologies, for example website analytics, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
